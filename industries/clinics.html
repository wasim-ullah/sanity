Where clinics lose patients

Most clinics don't need more traffic. They need fewer leaks.

Before spending more on ads, we look at what happens to the enquiries you already get. These three gaps come up again and again.

After hours

Missed calls and slow replies

Many enquiries arrive after reception closes or during the busiest hours. By the next morning, the patient has often booked somewhere else.

What we doAn instant WhatsApp reply offers booking times straight away. If the patient goes quiet, a polite follow-up goes out the next day.
Paid ads

Ads judged on clicks

Clicks and impressions look good in reports but do not fill appointments. Without booking data, budget keeps flowing to the wrong treatments.

What we doWe trace each booking back to the campaign that brought it. Budget then moves to the treatments that actually book.
Reputation

Reviews nobody asks for

Happy patients rarely leave a review unless asked at the right moment. Meanwhile, clinics with more reviews win the map results.

What we doA review request goes out after each visit, timed and worded to feel personal. Unhappy feedback comes to you first, so you can resolve it.

Questions

Questions we often get.

Can you help with health advertising approvals? +

In the UAE, medical ads usually need approval from the relevant health authority before they run. We prepare ads and landing pages with that process in mind and work with your team on the submission.

Do you work with single clinics or groups? +

Both. For groups, we set up reporting per branch and per treatment, so each manager sees their own numbers.

Will you handle patient records? +

No. Enquiries go to systems you control, such as your inbox, CRM or booking software, and we set up access on your accounts.

How soon will we see more bookings? +

Fixing reply times and tracking recovers enquiries you already get, so it is the fastest win. New campaigns and search work build on that over the following months.
