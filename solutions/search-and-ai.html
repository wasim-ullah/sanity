How it works

How the work runs each month.

Search is steady work rather than a one-off project. Each month follows the same rhythm, so you always know what was done and what comes next.

1.0Check

Find the gaps

We check rankings, your map listing and how AI assistants answer questions in your field. Gaps against competitors become the priorities.

2.0Fix

Fix the basics

Technical issues, missing pages and wrong business details are corrected. Small fixes here often unlock more than new content.

3.0Write

Publish and improve

New pages are written and weak ones improved, based on what buyers search for. Every page ends with a clear way to enquire.

4.0Earn

Build trust

Reviews, mentions and listings grow steadily on sites that matter. This is what makes Google and AI assistants confident to recommend you.

Questions

Questions we often get.

How long does search take to work? +

Fixes to a site and map listing can show within weeks. Competitive searches usually take several months of steady work, which is why we report on progress every month.

Can you guarantee first place on Google? +

No, and it is worth being careful with anyone who does. We can promise the work, the reporting and a clear view of what is moving.

How does a business get into AI answers? +

AI assistants lean on clear pages, consistent business details and mentions on sites they trust. We work on those same signals and check the answers each month.

Do you write the content? +

Yes. We write pages and articles based on what your buyers ask, and you approve everything before it goes live.
