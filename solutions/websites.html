How it works

From first call to live site.

The timeline depends on the number of pages and how quickly content is approved. You get a fixed scope, price and dates before any work starts.

1.0Discover

Plan the pages

We review your current site, your competitors and the searches your buyers make. You get a page plan and a list of what each page must answer.

2.0Write

Copy, then design

Words come first, so the design fits the message. You review real pages with real content, not empty templates.

3.0Build

Build and test

We build the site, connect forms and tracking, and test on phones and slow connections. Nothing goes live until every enquiry route works.

4.0Improve

Launch and learn

After launch we watch which pages bring enquiries and where people drop off. Fixes go in based on the numbers, not on opinions.

Measured on enquiries

Numbers you can act on.

We report on what brings in business, not on visits alone. Each month you get a short summary in plain language with the next fixes.

Enquiries by page

Which pages start calls, WhatsApp chats and form fills. This shows where to add content and what to cut.

Source of each lead

Search, ads, social or direct, saved with every enquiry. You can see which channel deserves more budget.

Booking rate

How many enquiries turn into a call or visit booked. This is the number that pays for the site.

Speed and errors

Load time on phones, plus any broken forms or links. Problems get fixed before they cost you leads.

Questions

Questions we often get.

Who owns the website? +

You do. The domain, content and design files sit in your name, and you get every login at handover.

What do you need from us? +

A kick-off call, plus access to your current site and analytics if you have them. After that, mostly quick approvals on the page plan, copy and design.

Can you redesign our current site instead? +

Yes, and it is often the better route. We first check what already brings enquiries, so we keep what works and rebuild what does not.

What happens after launch? +

We can keep improving the site each month, or hand it over fully to your team. Either way, your team can update text and images without a developer.
