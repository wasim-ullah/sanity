Careers

Do the best work of your career, without the agency noise.

SanityAI is a small team in Dubai working on websites, ads, search and automation for service businesses across the GCC. We hire slowly, and only when there is real work for someone good to own.

How we work

What working here feels like.

We would rather do fewer things properly than many things loudly. These are the habits we look for and try to protect.

01

Outcomes over output

We judge work by the enquiries and sales it brings, not by hours or slide counts. You will always know which number your work moves.

02

Plain words

We write the way we talk to clients, without jargon or filler. If an idea cannot be explained simply, it usually is not ready.

03

Honest numbers

We report what happened, including when something did not work. Nobody here gets in trouble for bad news shared early.

04

Ownership

You own your accounts and projects from start to finish. That comes with real trust and a direct line to the founder.

Who we want to hear from

Skills we are always open to.

We do not always have an open role. If your work is strong in one of these areas, we still want to see it, and we keep good people in mind.

Paid media

Google and Meta specialists who can show results measured on bookings or sales. LinkedIn and TikTok experience is a plus.

Search and content

Writers and SEO people who can explain a complex service in simple language. You care about readers first and rankings second.

Automation

Builders who know WhatsApp Business, CRMs and booking tools. You like making messy processes simple and reliable.

Design and web

Designers and developers who build fast, clean sites that work on a phone first. Taste matters, and so does the conversion rate.

How to apply

Three steps, no cover letter.

We care more about your work than your CV. The process is short, and you will hear back either way.

1.0Email

Send your work

Email work@sanity.ai with "Careers" and your area in the subject line. Include two or three pieces of work you are proud of and what you personally did on each.

2.0Talk

A real conversation

If there is a fit, we will set up a call to talk through your work. Expect questions about how you think, not trick questions.

3.0Try

A small paid task

For serious candidates, we agree a short, paid piece of work close to the real job. It shows both sides what working together is like.

4.0Decide

A clear answer

We make a decision quickly and tell you why. If it is not a fit now, we will say so honestly.
